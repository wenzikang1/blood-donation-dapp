## 📝 Important Notes

### About DOI Year vs. Publication Year Discrepancies

Some references show different years in their DOI compared to their publication year. This is **NORMAL** in academic publishing:

| Reference | DOI Year | Publication Year | Reason |
|-----------|----------|------------------|--------|
| [4] Ma et al. | 2022 | 2023 | Online-first publication |
| [10] Kuo et al. | 2023 | 2024 | Online-first publication |
| [12] Zhou et al. | 2023 | 2024 | Online-first publication |

**Explanation:**
- The DOI year typically represents when the article was accepted or published online
- The publication year may be later when it appears in a formal issue
- This follows standard international academic publishing practices
